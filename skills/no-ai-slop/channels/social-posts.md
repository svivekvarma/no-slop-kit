# Channel: social posts

LinkedIn, X, Threads, Bluesky.

This is the densest slop channel on the internet, because the form itself
rewards the patterns. The one-line-per-paragraph LinkedIn cadence, the hook, the
build, the mic-drop close: that shape is the slop. A post can follow every rule
of engagement writing and still be unreadable.

## Extra rules

- **Delete the hook line.** "I've been thinking about this a lot lately." "Nobody is talking about this." Start with the actual thing.
- **Delete the last line.** The kicker is the single most reliable slop tell in this channel. End on your last concrete sentence.
- **No one-sentence-per-line ladder** unless the content genuinely is a list. The staircase cadence is a format, not a thought.
- **Under 150 words.** Past 220 it is a blog post wearing a post's clothes.
- **No em dashes.**
- **At most a couple of emoji, and none as bullet markers.** Emoji as a list glyph is a signature of generated posts.
- **No engagement bait.** "Agree?" "Thoughts?" "Who else has noticed this?" "Save this for later." Delete.
- **No fake vulnerability arc.** "I failed. I learned. Now I help others." If the story is real, keep the specifics and cut the arc.
- **One claim, one example, done.** The example must be specific enough that it could only be yours.

## Channel-specific slop

| Slop | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Unpopular opinion:" | Just state the opinion. |
| "Let me be blunt." | Be blunt. |
| "This isn't about X. It's about Y." | Say Y. |
| "Read that again." | Delete. |
| "🚀 Excited to announce..." | "We shipped X. It does Y." |
| "The best engineers I know do 3 things:" | Name the one thing you actually observed, and who. |
| "What's your take? 👇" | Delete. |
| "And that changed everything." | Delete. |
| A closing standalone line of four words | Delete it. That is the kicker. |

## Keep

Keep the ugly specific number. Keep the thing you got wrong. Keep the opinion
that will annoy someone. Keep the post that is two sentences long, because two
honest sentences outperform the ladder.
