# Channel: social comments

Replies and comments on someone else's post, on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Hacker
News, YouTube.

A comment has one job: add something the post did not already say. The slop
failure here is the agreeable non-comment that praises the post, restates it,
and adds nothing. It reads as automated because it usually is.

## Extra rules

- **Add a fact, a disagreement, a counterexample or a question.** If the comment does none of those four, do not post it.
- **Never restate the post.** "So true, the key is that distribution matters more than product" is the post read back with approval.
- **Cut the compliment opener.** "Great post!" "This is such an important point." "Well said!" Delete and start with your substance.
- **Under 60 words.** A comment past 80 words is a post; make it one.
- **Reference the specific line you are responding to**, not the post in general.
- **No headings, no bullets, no bold.** One paragraph.
- **Disagree directly and without hedging theatre.** "I think this is wrong because X" beats "while I see the value here, I would gently push back".
- **No self-promotion bolted onto a comment.** If the plug is the point, the comment is an ad.

## Channel-specific slop

| Slop | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Great insights, thanks for sharing!" | Delete the whole comment or add a fact. |
| "Couldn't agree more!" then a restatement | Say the new thing, or say nothing. |
| "This resonates deeply." | Say which part and why. |
| "Spot on. 💯" | Delete. |
| "Adding to this: [generic list]" | Add the one item from your own experience. |
| "As someone who works in this space..." | Name the specific thing you saw. |
| "Would love to connect and discuss further!" | Delete. |
| "Thanks for the thoughtful breakdown, I especially liked point 3" | State what you think about point 3. |

## Keep

Keep the one-line correction. Keep "source?". Keep the blunt "this has not been
my experience, here is what happened instead". Keep the joke. A three-word
comment that lands is a real comment.
