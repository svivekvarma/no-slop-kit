# Channel: default

Use this when the draft does not belong to one of the nine named channels, or
when you genuinely cannot tell which it is.

There are no extra rules here. Apply the shared rules in `SKILL.md`: the editing
principles, the words to cut and the patterns to cut. The only channel setting
that applies is an em dash budget of roughly 1-2 per thousand words.

Before settling for this channel, check whether one of these fits, because each
one changes what counts as slop:

- `technical-docs` for anything a person reads while doing a task
- `articles-and-blogs` for anything with a byline and a reader who chose to read it
- `email` and `messages` for anything addressed to named people
- `social-posts` and `social-comments` for anything with an algorithm between you and the reader
- `code-comments` for anything inside a source file
- `pull-requests` and `review-comments` for anything attached to a diff

If the draft is genuinely mixed, edit the largest part in its own channel and
say in the What changed section which channel you applied.
